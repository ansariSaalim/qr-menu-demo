# MenuMitra demo

A clickable prototype of MenuMitra: QR menu and table ordering for restaurants. Diners scan the QR on their table, order and pay from their phone; the restaurant sees every order live.

- Web app (laptop, tablet, phone): https://ansarisaalim.github.io/qr-menu-demo/
- Phone app preview: https://ansarisaalim.github.io/qr-menu-demo/phone/

Use "View as" at the top to switch between a diner, the restaurant and our team. Use "Guided tour" to walk through the whole flow in 16 steps.

- **Diner:** browse restaurants, scan a table QR, order with portions and add-ons, track the order, pay by UPI or at the counter.
- **For restaurants:** the four plans (Freemium, Basic, Advanced, Premium), a full feature comparison, and a sign-up form.
- **Restaurant:** live orders with kitchen tickets; a menu editor with photos, categories, portions, add-ons, labels and a sold-out switch; table QR standees; reports; plan switching.
- **Our team:** every restaurant, plans, monthly revenue, and new sign-ups to approve.

**Every restaurant, dish, order and payment here is made-up sample data.** It is a front-end prototype only: nothing is sent to a server, and changes are kept only in your browser (use Reset to start again).

Built with React 18 and htm from a CDN, with no build step.
